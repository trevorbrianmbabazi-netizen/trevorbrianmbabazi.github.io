Consumer loan portfolio risk review

13,432 loans, UGX 24.0bn originated across four channels and four products. Headline default rate is 8.9%. That number is hiding a channel defaulting at twice the book average and two quarters of originations that should never have been written.

Vintage curves: cumulative default by months on book
Selected origination cohorts

Each line follows one origination cohort as it seasons, so cohorts are compared at the same age rather than the same calendar date. Everything written before mid 2025 converges around 8 to 10% by month six. The July to December 2025 cohorts break away immediately and sit 5 to 8 points higher at the same age. That is not a collections problem. Those loans were underwritten badly and the damage was locked in at origination.

Channel performance
Default rate and mean score

The agent network originates at a credit score 58 points below every other channel and defaults at roughly twice the rate. It is a third of the book.

Early payment default
Defaults within 3 months

Nearly half of agent network defaults happen inside three months. Borrowers who never make a meaningful payment point to origination quality or fraud, not to affordability.

Risk versus price
Realised default at mean APR

Subprime carries 4.8 times the default rate of prime but only 1.7 times the price. The band is being written below its risk adjusted cost.
