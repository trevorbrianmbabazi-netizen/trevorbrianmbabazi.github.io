Nsimbi Resilience Programme

Eight quarters of delivery across eight districts and four implementing partners. Two thirds of the programme period has elapsed. Reach is close to plan overall, but the portfolio average is hiding two districts and one output that are materially off track.

District attainment against target

Six districts are within reach of plan. Kotido has delivered under half its target and Amudat is a distant seventh. Those same two districts are the only ones where female participation sits below 40%, and they carry the highest cost per beneficiary in the programme. The underperformance is expensive as well as incomplete.
